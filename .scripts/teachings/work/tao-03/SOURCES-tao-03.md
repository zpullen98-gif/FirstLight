## The Tao Te Ching, days 55 to 81 (tao-03)

Fifty four candidates were proposed, a primary and a standby for each of the twenty seven days, in five lanes. All twenty seven primaries were judged: twenty six as VERIFIED and one, day 55, as CORRECTED. Each was then attacked by a refuter who opened the packet source again, and on several days (67 to 72) fetched the live Wikisource revision himself; none was refuted. The critic then read the batch whole and raised three problems, on days 70, 72 and 74; each was sent to revision, each revision was judged CORRECTED, and each revised entry was attacked again by a fresh refuter and stood. Status gives twenty seven living primaries, and all twenty seven survive and are used. The twenty seven standbys were never verified, because no primary fell. The editor changed nothing in any entry: every entry is copied exactly as status.js gives its survivor (for days 70, 72 and 74, the revised entry), and none held a straight quote to be made curly.

The spread: Wang Bi is named on thirteen days (56, 57, 58, 61, 63, 65, 69, 70, 72, 73, 75, 77 and 79) and the Heshang Gong commentary on fourteen (55, 59, 60, 62, 64, 66, 67, 68, 71, 74, 76, 78, 80 and 81). No day names two commentators. No entry is hedged: every reading is taken from the commentator's own Chinese on zh.wikisource, Wang Bi from revision 2354026 (the Huating Zhang edition, 2024) and the Heshang Gong commentary from revision 1552800 (chapters 38 to 81, a normalised text, 2019), the page packet.js names for these chapters, and every refuter confirmed that the base line commented on is the line of the day's key verse. Legge's notes were used only as a check, on day 74, where they independently report the Heshang Gong commentary's gloss of the One who presides over killing as Heaven. Day 66 names the Heshang Gong commentary because Wang Bi has no comment on chapter 66; on day 78 Wang Bi has no comment on the key line.

The CORRECTED entries. Day 55, at verification: the verifier changed the plain sense from "the newborn's soft bones" to "the infant's weak bones", since the chapter has the bones weak and the sinews soft, and says infant. The reading, the key verse and the source line were left as proposed. Days 70, 72 and 74 are CORRECTED by revision, and are set out in the next paragraph.

The revisions. Day 70: the critic found that the gapped clause "keeps men from knowing and glory and profit from practising" first parses as one list, knowing and glory and profit. The revision gives each verb its object and marks the gap with a comma, "keeps men from knowing them, and glory and profit from practising them", and to stay within forty five words "without going out of doors" becomes "without leaving home", which still sits within Wang Bi's 可不出戶窺牖而知. The reading, the key verse, the plain sense and the source are unchanged. Day 72: the word warning stood on both sides of the semicolon ("the verse gives a warning; Wang Bi ... takes it as warning that"). The revision drops only the second, so that Wang Bi now "takes it to mean that" once stillness and humility give way to reliance on might, might no longer holds the people and Heaven's punishment comes; the reading and its evidence are unchanged. Day 74, a hard passage on killing: the critic found that the reading stopped at the ruler labouring to no effect, while the Heshang Gong commentary sets its own limit on the line just after the key verse (夫代大匠斲者，希有不傷手矣), saying that the ruler who punishes in Heaven's place misses the pattern and harms himself (還自傷). Rule 6 and the plan trap require that limit to be carried. The revision ends the reading "toiling in vain and wounding himself", adds 還自傷 to the evidence, trims "watching over" to "watching" (司察) and turns "a clumsy hand" into "a clumsy workman" (拙夫) to keep forty five words. The commentator, the key verse and the plain sense are unchanged. The second refuter on day 74 noted that the commentary's earlier concession (乃應王法執而殺之) sits on the previous line and is not a limit on the key verse.

Reservations the refuters recorded and judged minor, not misstatements. Day 61: "stooping only to keep itself whole" reads Wang Bi's 自全而已 as result rather than purpose, which the English allows; the sentence stands at forty five words, the cap. Day 64: the reading carries the comment on 學不學 and leaves aside the comment on the desire half of the verse (欲不欲). Day 66: "so" hangs the people's love as for parents on both clauses, where the commentary gives it to the second. Day 72: "Heaven's punishment comes" slightly flattens 天誅將至, is about to come; the second refuter also noted that Legge follows the Heshang Gong gloss of this line (威，害也), but Wang Bi's reading is his own and correctly attributed. Day 75: Wang Bi has a single comment after the whole chapter, and the sentence says he reads the chapter, not the line; the refuter noted that the Heshang Gong comment on this very line (以其君上多欲，好有為也) is more closely tied to the key verse, but Wang Bi's reading is his own and found at the place named, so it was kept. Day 79: "settled" smooths 明理, and "the grudge as a wound" compresses his chain of the unhealed injury and the grudge that follows from it. Day 69 is a hard passage on war: the plain sense states the armies meeting plainly, and the reading carries Wang Bi's own limit, not chasing gain.

The holes. There are none: all twenty seven days are used.

The gate. Run as the brief gives it, the candidate gate joins this batch to the set already landed and now passes on fifty four days with no error and no warning; the errors on days 1 to 27 reported at the first edit are gone from the set.

Traps. Three observations that may belong in traps.md. From day 77: the packet can group a chapter's closing lines under one comment block of Wang Bi's; find which sentence of the block glosses which line (here 唯其道也 closes the gloss on 唯有道者, and the next sentence belongs to the sage line) before building a reading on it. From day 63: where Wang Bi comments once on a run of lines, a closing 故曰 quoting the line fixes the place his comment belongs to. From day 74, found by the critic after two passes missed it: on a hard passage the commentary's limit may be spread across two comments, a first that only says the ruler's effort fails (勞而無功) and a second, on the line after the key verse, that says he harms himself (還自傷); a reading that stops at the first has not carried the limit.
