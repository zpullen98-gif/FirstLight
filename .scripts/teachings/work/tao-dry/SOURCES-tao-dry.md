## The Tao Te Ching, days 1 to 31 (tao-dry)

This was a dry run of the tao plan over three days, 1, 8 and 31, and it will not land. Six candidates were proposed, a primary and a standby for each day. The three primaries were verified, day 1 as VERIFIED, day 8 as CORRECTED and day 31 as VERIFIED, and each was then attacked by a refuter who reopened the packet source; none was refuted. The critic then asked for two revisions, on days 8 and 31; both were made, both revised entries were attacked again by a second refuter who reopened the raw text of the Wikisource revision, and neither was refuted. All three primaries survived and all three are used, two of them in their revised form. The three standbys were never verified, because no primary fell. The batch passed the gate with no error and no warning, and the editor changed nothing in any entry.

The spread: Wang Bi is named on one day (day 1, chapter 1, his comment on the nameless and the named) and the Heshang Gong commentary on two (day 8, chapter 8, and day 31, chapter 31). No entry is hedged; every reading is taken from the commentator's own Chinese on zh.wikisource, Wang Bi from revision 2354026 (the Huating Zhang edition, 2024) and the Heshang Gong commentary from revision 2019760 (a normalised text, 2021), both as the packet gives them.

Day 1 stands as verified. Wang Bi comments on exactly the key verse's line, 無名，天地之始，有名，萬物之母, and the reading rests on his words 凡有皆始於無 (all that exists begins in what has no being), on the formless and nameless stage as the beginning and the named stage as the mother that grows and rears things, and on 道以無形無名始成萬物. The plain sense does not lean on Legge's supplied "(conceived of as)".

CORRECTED, day 8, the primary, at verification. The proposal read the Heshang Gong commentary as taking the highest excellence "as a person with water's nature". The commentary says 上善之人，如水之性, the person of highest goodness is like the nature of water, which is a likeness and not an identity, so the verifier changed the words to "a person whose nature is like water's". Wang Bi's comment on chapter 8 is slight, which is why the commentary is named there.

Revision, day 8. The critic found that the closing images, stopping when dammed and flowing when released, were left without their point: in the commentary they gloss 夫唯不爭 and end in 聽從人也, it goes along with people, which the sentence did not say. The sentence also used "takes" twice and "the highest excellence" twice. The reviser adopted the critic's fix with two changes of his own: "its not striving" became "water's not striving", since "its" was ambiguous, and "yielding to" became "going along with", which renders 聽從 more closely. "Occupies" now stands in the plain sense, as in Legge. The clause on water giving all things life (萬物得水以生) was dropped, and the evidence narrowed to 上善之人，如水之性 and 壅之則止，決之則流，聽從人也. The second refuter confirmed that the paraphrase belongs to the later 不爭 line, not the earlier one, and that 水性如是 on the next line bears out that the not striving is water's.

Revision, day 31. Day 31 is a hard passage (killing in war and the rites of mourning). Wang Bi has no comment on chapter 31 in the received text, so the Heshang Gong commentary is named. As verified, the entry gave only the commentary's reading of 殺人之眾，以哀悲泣之: grief that the victor's own virtue was too thin to transform people by the Tao (傷己德薄，不能以道化人). The refuter, the dry run's first critic and the critic of this round all noted that the key verse also holds 戰勝，以喪禮處之, and that the commentary's comment on that very line sets a limit, 明君子貴德而賤兵，不得以而誅不祥: the gentleman prizes virtue, despises arms and punishes only when he cannot avoid it. Rule 6 asks for that limit, so the revision added it, rendering 誅 as "punishes" rather than the critic's broader "arms are used", and shortened the plain sense ("by mourning rites") to stay within the word limit. The clause on harm to the innocent (而害無辜之民) fell out to make room; the second refuter noted that it could return if the cap allows, but its absence claims nothing beyond the text. The plain sense states the killing and the mourning in one plain clause and does not lean on Legge's supplied "(rightly)".

Evidence. The operator's machine recheck found every primary's evidence in its source, but it ran before the revisions. The editor matched the revised evidence of days 8 and 31 against the packet text, by the recheck's own rule, and every piece was found; the recheck should still be run again on the revised entries before any real batch built on these chapters lands.

There are no holes.

Traps. Two from this run are already in traps.md: the Heshang Gong commentary sits on two Wikisource pages (revision 2019760 for chapters 1 to 37, revision 1552800 for chapters 38 to 81), so a day must cite the page the packet names; and on a hard passage the commentary's limit may sit on a line inside the key verse or just after it, so the comment on every line of the key verse and the line after must be read before the reading is written. One more belongs there: in chapter 8 the words 不爭 occur twice (水善利萬物而不爭 and 夫唯不爭), and the Heshang Gong commentary glosses them differently (dew and springs on the first, damming and release on the second), so a reading of the chapter's not striving must be built on the comment to the line it names.
