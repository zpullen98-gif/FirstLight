## The Tao Te Ching, days 1 to 27 (tao-01)

Fifty four candidates were proposed, a primary and a standby for each of the twenty seven days, in five lanes. All twenty seven primaries were verified, every one as VERIFIED at first judgement, and each was then attacked by a refuter who reopened the packet source and, in most cases, fetched the raw Wikisource revision himself. One primary was refuted in that first round, day 2, on its plain sense alone; it was judged again, CORRECTED, and lived. After the first edit the critic raised one problem, on day 21, and that entry was revised and not refuted. The batch then landed as a preview. After it landed the owner amended the rules (26 September 2026): a sentence of at most 45 words where one commentator is named, and a plain sense that gives the chapter's context and repeats no four words in a row of the key verse. Every one of the twenty seven landed entries broke the new rules, so every one was sent back for revision; twenty seven revisions were made, each was attacked again by a second refuter, twenty six stood and one, day 5, was refuted. The critic of the next edit then raised three problems, and five further revisions answered them (days 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8); a refuter reopened the packet for each and refuted none. In all thirty three revisions were made and one was refuted. Status now gives twenty seven living primaries, each in its latest standing revision, and all twenty seven are used. The twenty seven standbys were never verified, because no primary fell. The editor changed nothing in any entry: every entry is copied exactly as status.js gives its survivor, and none held a straight quote to be made curly.

The spread: Wang Bi is named on fourteen days (1, 2, 5, 10, 11, 12, 14, 16, 17, 21, 22, 23, 25 and 27) and the Heshang Gong commentary on thirteen (3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 13, 15, 18, 19, 20, 24 and 26). No day names two commentators. No entry is hedged: every reading is taken from the commentator's own Chinese on zh.wikisource, Wang Bi from revision 2354026 (the Huating Zhang edition, 2024) and the Heshang Gong commentary from revision 2019760 (chapters 1 to 37, a normalised text, 2021), both as the packet gives them, and every refuter confirmed that the base line commented on is the line of the day's key verse. Legge's notes were used only as a check, on days 12 and 13, where they independently report the same comment. Every piece of evidence was found again by the machine recheck.

The revisions under the amended rules. On every day the key verse, its ref and place and the commentator are unchanged; the plain sense was rewritten as context only, and the reading was shortened within the commentator's own words, with nothing added. So every entry now stands as CORRECTED by revision. What each shortening left out: day 1 keeps Wang Bi's comment on the key verse's own line and takes "two aspects" from the chapter's own wording; day 2 keeps the reading whole and its plain sense now names the chapter's pairs of opposites, leaning on nothing Legge supplied; day 3 drops the comment on filling the belly (懷道抱一); day 4 drops the clause on following the Tao that does not display itself (法道不自見), which a refuter had called a soft rendering; day 6 drops the clause on heaven and earth feeding a person; day 7 drops the world's honouring of the sage; day 8 uses the comment on the second 不爭 (夫唯不爭), not the first, and adds the frame 上善之人，如水之性 to the evidence as the warrant for reading the not wrangling as water's way; day 9 drops the phrase on Heaven's constant way; day 10 drops the clause on things growing of themselves; day 11 leaves out 以實, a crux that some editions give as 以寡; day 12 leaves the conclusion 故聖人不為目 implicit; day 13 keeps all four of the commentary's clauses; day 14 keeps all three of Wang Bi's; day 15 drops growing daily emptier and not being wilful; day 16 keeps its three clauses; day 17 drops the orders that are not followed; day 18 drops "in every house" and loyalty; day 19 drops the knotted cords; day 20 narrows "form or sign" to "sign"; day 22 keeps all four comments; day 23 keeps the gloss of 希 as what is listened for and not heard; day 24 compresses 立身行道; day 25 drops the square and round illustration; day 26 drops the plant and the dragon and tiger analogies; day 27 keeps Wang Bi's three clauses. The second refuters recorded reservations they judged minor and not misstatements: on day 19, that "the sages' making" is thin English for 制作; on day 25, that "links four in a chain" is a description of the chain and not his words; on day 27, that Wang Bi's comment sits on the saving of men, and the saving of things gets no gloss of its own, though his 萬物 and 物 cover it.

The revisions of the second edit. Day 5 had been left a hole in the first edit, because its revision under the amended rules was refuted for drift: it rendered 任自然 as heaven and earth "following what is so of itself", whereas 任 is to leave to, give free rein to, and Wang Bi's point is that heaven and earth leave all things to what they are of themselves, as his next clause, 萬物自相治理, spells out. The critic held that a living primary should not stand as a hole while the landed preview kept its old sentence, which fails the amended rules, and a new revision was made on the refuter's own suggestion: the plain sense now says only that the chapter opens with the verse and then says the same of the sages, and Wang Bi is read as having heaven and earth make nothing and leave all things to order themselves, with his example that earth grows no grass for beasts, yet beasts eat grass, grounded in 天地任自然，無為無造，萬物自相治理 and 地不為獸生芻，而獸食芻. It was not refuted; the refuter noted only that "making nothing" compresses 無為無造 and leaves out the acting, an omission and not a misstatement. Day 8's plain sense had been a participle with no main clause ("Closing a chapter that likens the highest excellence to water"), so that grammatically the commentary closed the chapter; it was given a subject and verb ("The verse closes a chapter") and the reading was left as it was. Days 3, 4 and 6 had cited the Heshang Gong edition as "zh.wikisource, revision 2019760 (2021)" while days 7 to 26 said "zh.wikisource (a normalised text), revision 2019760 (2021)", as the packet describes it; the three source lines now carry "(a normalised text)" too, so the edition actually read is named alike on every Heshang Gong day. Nothing else in those three entries changed.

Day 21 was revised twice, both times before the second edit. The first revision answered the first critic: the reading had ended on "takes 'this' as what has been said above", a pointer with no content, and the reviser went back to Wang Bi's comments on the lines just before 以此 (至真之極，不可得名，無名則是其名也。自古及今，無不由此而成 and 眾甫，物之始也。以無名說萬物始也), so that 'this' became the account above of the nameless, through which all is completed. That entry ran to sixty words and its plain sense echoed the key verse, so the second revision rewrote the plain sense as context (the close of a chapter on the elusive Tao, a question and its answer) and kept the reading's content. A refuter reopened the packet and the raw revision after each and refuted neither; the last noted that the passive "how all things are known" loses the first person of the question, a minor loss.

Day 2 had been CORRECTED once before, after the first refutation: its plain sense had turned Legge's supplied "(the idea of)" into the verb of the sentence, stating the translator's reading as the chapter's. The revision under the amended rules removed the problem at its root, since the plain sense no longer paraphrases the verse at all.

The holes. There are none: all twenty seven days are used, day 5 included.

The gate. Run as the brief gives it, the candidate gate reports every day as already landed and every key as given twice, and it counts the errors of the old preview entries (too long, and plain senses that repeat the key) against the set; those errors belong to the preview still in the set, not to this batch, and no entry of this batch is named in them. Checked with the twenty seven days of this batch taken back from the set, which is what the landing script does with its replace option, the whole set passes with no error and no warning. The batch must therefore be landed with the replace option.

Traps. The two traps from the first edit are in traps.md: a parenthesis of Legge's turned into the verb of the plain sense (day 2), and a comment that points back is not a reading in itself (day 21). Two more belong there. From day 5: 任 in Wang Bi's 任自然 is to leave things to themselves, not to follow; a reading that makes heaven and earth, or the sage, the one who follows what is so of itself moves Wang Bi's non interference towards imitation of nature, which is nearer the Heshang Gong commentary's idiom than his. Render 任 as leaving to, or letting be. From day 8: a plain sense written as a participle with no main clause, ending at the semicolon, attaches itself to the commentator named after it; give the plain sense its own subject and verb. A further observation, not yet a trap: where Legge's English and the commentator's gloss of the same characters part (day 21, 眾甫, which Legge renders as the beauties of existing things; day 23, 希言, which Legge renders as abstaining from speech and Wang Bi as speech listened for and not heard), the plain sense stays with the chapter and the reading gives the commentator's sense as his, without resolving the two.
