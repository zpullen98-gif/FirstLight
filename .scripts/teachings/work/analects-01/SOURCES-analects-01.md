## The Analects, days 1 to 21 (analects-01)

Forty two candidates were proposed, a primary and a standby for each of the twenty one days, in four lanes. All twenty one primaries were judged: twenty as VERIFIED at first judgement and one, day 8, as CORRECTED. Each was then attacked by a refuter who reopened the packet source, and on days 1 to 6 and 16 to 18 fetched the raw Wikisource revision as well and dated it by the Wikisource API. One primary was refuted, day 11, on form alone; it was judged again, CORRECTED, and lived. A critic then read all twenty one survivors together and raised two problems, on days 3 and 6; both went to revision, both revisions were judged CORRECTED, and a second refuter reopened each source and refuted neither. Status gives twenty one living primaries, and all twenty one survive and are used. The twenty one standbys were never verified, because no primary fell. The editor changed nothing in any entry: every entry is copied exactly as status.js gives its survivor (for day 11 the entry as corrected at the second judgement, for days 3 and 6 the entry as revised), and none held a straight quote to be made curly.

The spread: Zhu Xi is named on eleven days (1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 12, 13, 15, 17, 19 and 21) and He Yan on ten (2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 11, 14, 16, 18 and 20). Of the He Yan days, six take an untagged note, which the plan’s rule makes He Yan’s own (days 2, 6, 8, 9, 16 and 18), and four take a tagged gloss, credited through “He Yan’s collection” to the scholar it names: Kong Anguo on day 4 (孔曰), Chen Qun on day 11 (陳曰) and Ma Rong on days 14 and 20 (馬曰). No day names two commentators. No entry is hedged: every reading is taken from the commentator’s own Chinese on zh.wikisource, as the packet gives it, Zhu Xi from the Sishu zhangju jizhu pages of the Lunyu jizhu and He Yan from the proofread Lunyu jijie pages for books 1 to 6 and from the Jijie notes in the Lunyu zhushu for books 7 to 20, each at the revision its source line names, and every refuter confirmed that the comment used sits on the line the key verse holds. Legge’s notes were used only as a check: on day 11 his note on 11.4 names Chen Qun as given in He Yan, and his note on 11.11 reports that the older interpreters took the replies as declining to answer. On day 10 He Yan’s collection gives Zheng Xuan’s 重人賤畜 to the same effect as Zhu Xi, and the sentence names Zhu Xi alone. On day 13 He Yan has no gloss on the line of the key verse, and on day 20 Zhu Xi’s note on it is Xie’s (謝氏曰), not his own, so the other commentator is named on each.

The corrected entries. Day 8, at verification: the proposal called 8.16 a lament, but in day.js 8.16 censures the ardent who are not upright, the stupid who are not attentive and the simple who are not sincere, which is not grief. The plain sense now reads “a saying on the ardent who are not upright”; the reading, the key verse and the source line are unchanged, and the entry stands at forty five words, the cap. Day 11, after refutation: the substance held (the only Jijie note on 11.11 is 陳曰：鬼神及死事難明，語之無益，故不答, verbatim the evidence), but the sentence said Chen Qun “reads them as withholding an answer”, and “them” could only refer to Chi Lu’s two questions or to the spirits of the dead, neither of which withholds anything; the replies, which are what 故不答 is about, were never named. The second judgement changed only those words, to “takes both replies as declining to answer”, which is exactly 故不答. Zhu Xi on the same chapter reads the replies as a deep answer and not a refusal; the entry names only Chen Qun, so no disagreement is resolved. Days 3 and 6 were also CORRECTED, through revision, as the next paragraph gives.

The revisions. Two, both from the critic, and both kept after a second refutation. Day 3: the plain sense named only “a proverb on courting the furnace”, so the reading’s “corner or furnace” brought in a corner the reader had not met; and the close, “one who defies principle cannot pray his way out, and should flatter neither”, pinned the advice to court neither on the offender, whereas Zhu Xi gives it as the general rule (言但當順理，非特不當媚竈，亦不可媚於奧也: he means one should simply follow principle, and flatter neither the furnace nor the corner). The plain sense now names the proverb as preferring the furnace to the corner, as Legge’s 3.13 sets them, and the reading now runs: Heaven as principle, no prayer to corner or furnace clears one who defies it, so one should follow principle and court neither. The commentator, the place, the key verse and the source line are unchanged, and the entry stands at forty five words. Day 6: “Asked by the Duke Ai who loved learning” read first as a description of the Duke, not as his question. It now reads “Asked by the Duke Ai which disciple loved learning”, which follows Legge’s own “which of the disciples”; only that opening clause changed, the reading from He Yan’s untagged note is as before, and the entry stands at forty four words. The revision refuters reopened both sources on zh.wikisource (the revisions dated 2021 and 2023 by the API) and found no drift from the evidence.

Reservations the refuters recorded and judged minor, not misstatements. Day 2: “for” makes causal a link that He Yan’s note only sets side by side. Day 7: 素行合於神明 also stands in Kong Anguo’s gloss in He Yan’s collection, but Zhu Xi adopts it in his own text, so the attribution holds. Day 9: “after praising” gives only half of what precedes, since 9.26 ends with the Master checking Chi Lu for repeating the ode. Day 12: “whose recovery” may take either antecedent, which Zhu Xi’s equation of the heart’s whole virtue with heavenly principle allows. Day 13, a passage on law: “a son who testifies” singularises the Duke’s conditional plural, though it matches the Chinese, and “shielding” is a mild word for 隱, though the key verse printed above says “conceals”. Day 17: “retainers” is a fair English for Legge’s subsidiary ministers but not the text’s term. Day 20, a passage on law: “long” leans on Legge’s trailing “for a long time”. Day 21: Zhu Xi takes T’ang’s address as spoken to the princes, but the plain sense follows the translation’s own terms. Day 19: the revision year was taken as the batch gives it and not dated again. The reservation first recorded on day 3, that its close took the offender as its subject, is the one the critic raised and the revision cured. The refuters’ optional wordings were not applied, since an entry may change only through a revision.

The holes. There are none: all twenty one days are used.

The gate. Run as the brief gives it, the candidate gate passes on twenty one days with no error and no warning. No day had to be withdrawn.

Traps. Five observations may belong in traps.md. From day 11: when a gloss is about what the speaker does (故不答, he did not answer), the reading must name the speaker’s replies as its object; a pronoun that can only point back to the question or to its subject garbles the reading at six in the morning. From day 8: a plain sense that names the unit before or after the key verse must give that unit’s own tone as day.js prints it; a censure is not a lament. From days 3 and 6, the critic’s two: an image the reading uses (the corner beside the furnace) must already stand in the plain sense, and a commentator’s general rule (言但當順理) must not be pinned grammatically on the one person the sentence has just named; and an opening “Asked by X who loved learning” reads as a description of X, so the question needs its own word (“which”). From days 9, 12 and 20: Zhu Xi’s Jizhu, like He Yan’s collection, quotes earlier scholars under their names (程子曰, 謝氏曰, 范氏曰), often after a circle; only his untagged comment is his own, and a day whose only note on the key line is a quoted scholar’s cannot name Zhu Xi for it. From days 14 and 18, two more cases of the observation already made in the Tao Te Ching batches, that where Legge’s English and the commentator’s gloss part, the plain sense stays with the chapter and the reading gives the commentator’s sense as his: on day 14 Legge’s “without modesty” against Ma Rong’s unashamed speech from substance within, and on day 18 Legge’s gourd hung up “out of the way of being eaten” against He Yan’s gourd that does not eat. The plan’s trap on Legge’s double hyphen held: on day 16 it sits in the reply of the unit before the key verse, and on day 19 earlier in the key verse’s own unit, and each key verse was taken from a sentence that stands clear of it.
